About us

TNP Go is a placement preparation application designed to help students practice and improve their skills in aptitude, technical, and English assessments. The application provides a simple and user-friendly platform where students can attempt practice questions, test their knowledge, and build confidence for placement examinations. Currently, each section contains 10 practice questions, covering different concepts and difficulty levels. The application is currently under development, with plans to add more questions, topics, features, and improvements in future.
